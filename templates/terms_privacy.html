Terms & Privacy

Please read the following terms and privacy conditions before using SafeScan AI.

1. About SafeScan AI

SafeScan AI is an AI-assisted digital security analysis platform designed to help users examine potentially suspicious emails, URLs, QR codes, and other digital content. The platform provides technical and evidence-based information to help users understand possible security risks before interacting with unknown or suspicious digital content.

2. Purpose of the Platform

SafeScan AI is developed as a digital safety and security analysis tool. Its purpose is to provide users with additional technical information such as domain details, DNS information, IP information, SSL certificate details, redirects, website metadata, security headers, and other available indicators that may help users make safer decisions.

3. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for the information and files they submit to SafeScan AI. Users should only submit content that they are authorized to analyze. SafeScan AI should be used as an assistance tool and users should independently verify important information before making security, financial, or other critical decisions.

4. Email and Personal Messages

Users may submit suspicious emails or messages to the MailGuard module for analysis. These messages may contain sender addresses, domains, links, message text, and other information required for security investigation. Users should avoid submitting passwords, OTPs, authentication codes, private keys, or other highly sensitive information.

5. URL Analysis

The URLShield module allows users to submit website URLs for technical investigation. SafeScan AI may examine the submitted URL, domain information, redirects, DNS records, IP information, SSL certificates, page metadata, security headers, technologies, and other publicly available technical information required for the analysis.

6. QR Code Analysis

The QRShield module allows users to upload QR-code images for analysis. If a QR code contains a URL, SafeScan AI may decode the destination and perform additional URL and domain investigation. QR codes may contain payment links, personal URLs, contact information, or other data, so users should carefully review the result before opening or interacting with the destination.

7. Data Processing

Information submitted to SafeScan AI may be processed by the application to generate security-analysis results. Depending on the module and implementation, submitted information and analysis results may be handled by the application's processing logic and stored as analysis history for the application's functionality.

8. Analysis History

SafeScan AI may store certain analysis information in its local application database to maintain analysis history. The stored information can include the module used, submitted input or relevant input data, verdict, findings, metadata, and analysis time. Users should therefore avoid submitting information that they do not want to be retained by the application.

9. Third-Party Technical Services

Some technical investigations may rely on external services or public technical databases to obtain information such as domain registration details, DNS information, IP registration information, or threat-intelligence information. When such services are used, relevant technical information may be sent to those services as required to perform the requested investigation.

10. Personal and Financial Information

SafeScan AI is not intended to collect or process highly sensitive information such as passwords, OTPs, banking credentials, card numbers, private keys, or authentication tokens. Users should remove or avoid submitting such information whenever possible, especially when analyzing personal emails, payment-related QR codes, or private messages.

11. Security Analysis Results

SafeScan AI provides evidence-based analysis based on the information available during the investigation. A result such as Likely Legitimate, Suspicious, High Risk, or Unable to Verify should not be considered an absolute guarantee that a website, message, QR code, or URL is safe or malicious. Security conditions can change, and some information may not be publicly available.

12. Unable to Verify

When sufficient technical evidence is not available to make a reliable conclusion, SafeScan AI may return Unable to Verify instead of making an unsupported determination. This helps prevent the system from treating missing information as proof that something is safe or malicious.

13. No Guaranteed Protection

SafeScan AI is an assistance and analysis platform and cannot guarantee complete protection against phishing, fraud, malware, impersonation, scams, or other cyber threats. Users should continue to follow normal security practices and verify important information through trusted sources.

14. External Websites and Links

SafeScan AI may identify and analyze URLs that lead to external websites. SafeScan AI does not control the content, security, availability, or privacy practices of external websites. Users should exercise caution before opening external links or providing information to third-party websites.

15. Responsible Use

Users must use SafeScan AI only for lawful and responsible security analysis. The platform should not be used to access unauthorized accounts, obtain private information, bypass security controls, distribute malicious content, or perform activities that may harm other individuals or systems.

16. Privacy and Data Protection

SafeScan AI is designed to process submitted information only for the purpose of performing the requested analysis and supporting application functionality. Users should understand what information they submit and should avoid providing unnecessary personal or confidential information.

17. Changes to These Conditions

These Terms & Privacy conditions may be updated when SafeScan AI's features, data-processing methods, or technical services change. Users are encouraged to review the page periodically when using a newer version of the application.

18. Acceptance

By using SafeScan AI and submitting content for analysis, users acknowledge that they understand these Terms & Privacy conditions and agree to use the platform responsibly. If a user does not agree with these conditions, they should not submit content to the application.
